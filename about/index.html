About

Governor Helena Foulkes

Governor Helena Buonanno Foulkes is a lifelong Rhode Islander, wife of 36 years, mother of four, grandmother, and cancer survivor. She rose through the ranks of CVS over 25 years to become president of its retail division.

As President of CVS Pharmacy, Helena worked to get consumers the lowest possible prescription drug prices. She removed tobacco products from the shelves, even though tobacco brought in $2 billion in revenue, because protecting people's health matters more than profits.

Before taking office, Helena partnered with the mayor of Central Falls to improve its public schools, and fought homelessness and domestic violence as a board member and volunteer at nonprofits.

As Governor, Helena is focused on making state government work for every Rhode Islander. Bio to be updated
